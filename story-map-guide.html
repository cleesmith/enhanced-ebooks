Story Map

A visual view of your whole story. Every passage is a card, every choice is an arrow, and you can arrange, link, and edit it all on one canvas.

Opening it

From the Passages editor, click Story Map in the top-right corner. To return, click ← Passages on the map toolbar. The Load, Save, and Generate & Download buttons stay available the whole time, so you never have to leave the map to save or export.

Reading the map

Each card is a single passage, showing its id, a small ▶ if it includes a video, and a preview of its text. Choices appear as curved arrows from one card to another. The green rocket marks where the story begins — the passage whose id is start.

Getting around

Drag any empty part of the canvas to pan, or scroll to move. Zoom with the − and + buttons or by holding Ctrl / ⌘ while scrolling, and use Fit to frame the entire story at once. Zoom far out and the cards simplify to just their titles.

Adding a passage

Click + New. A fresh passage drops into the middle of the view, already selected and ready to edit. (Passages can still be created from the Passages list too — both build the same story.)

Arranging passages

Drag a card to move it. Where you place it is remembered and saved with your project, so the layout you design comes back exactly as you left it. The arrows follow as you move.

Linking passages

Each card has a small dot on its right edge. Drag from that dot onto another card to create a link — this adds a choice in the first passage that leads to the second.

Removing a link

Hover over a connecting arrow and a small ✕ appears at its middle. Click it to remove that choice.

Editing a passage

Double-click a card, or select it and click Edit. A dialog opens right over the map with every field: passage id, YouTube video id, heading, narrative text, and the full list of choices. Close it with Done, the Esc key, or by clicking outside it — the map stays open behind it.

Deleting a passage

Select a card and click Delete, or press the Delete key. You’ll be asked to confirm first.

Spotting broken links

A red dashed line means a choice points to a passage that no longer exists — usually after a rename or delete. Open that passage and fix or remove the choice, and the warning clears.

Good to know

To link two passages, the target must already exist — create it first, then connect to it. You can’t drop a wire on empty canvas to make a passage.

A new link starts without a label. Add the words the reader will click by editing the passage.

The entry point is whichever passage has the id start. A passage becomes the beginning simply by being named start.
